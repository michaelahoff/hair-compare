## Broader subscription benchmarks

RevenueCat's 2026 report covers over 115,000 apps using its platform, mainly 2025 performance data. It is a selected subscription-app sample, not all apps or this specific niche. [Methodology and report](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)

- Health & Fitness median prices: $9.99 monthly, approximately $39.94 annual.
- North American median prices across categories: $9.99 monthly, $39.99 annual.
- Health & Fitness median trial-to-paid conversion: 37.7%; download-to-paid by day 35: 2.9%.
- AI apps produce 41% more revenue per payer but churn 30% faster in the report's aggregate comparison.

All four benchmarks come from [RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps). These are reference points, not forecasts. The page's prose contains conflicting statements about category plan-duration mix, so this note does not use its annual/monthly share claims.

## Implications for Hair Compare

The following is product judgment inferred from the evidence, rather than sourced market facts:

- Keep free local photo capture/import, alignment, synchronized comparison/zoom, treatment dates and export useful indefinitely. This gives users a reason to build a photo history before buying AI.
- Sell recurring interpretation of that history: scalp assessments, comparison reports, trend explanations and optional cloud backup. The strongest reason to renew is useful longitudinal insight, rather than a single score.
- **$5.99–$7.99/month** is a defensible launch test below the Health & Fitness median and between low-price direct competitors and $9.99 AI offerings. **$9.99/month** is plausible after demonstrating reliable analysis and repeated value; current evidence does not prove it will convert better.
- Start annual testing around **$39.99–$49.99/year**, subject to measured service costs. That is close to annual market benchmarks and $44.99 direct AI competitors. Public $19.99/year competitors are a real lower anchor, even though their current offer eligibility is unknown.
- Monthly and annual plans can unlock the same feature set. State an explicit monthly analysis allowance rather than promising unlimited calls before observing real usage and abuse costs. All *types* of analysis can be included without unlimited *volume*.
- Avoid lifetime unlimited cloud AI: recurring computation and storage costs continue after the purchase. A lifetime product could be appropriate for device-only features later.
- A 7-day paid-tier trial or sample report can demonstrate the AI while the ongoing free tier remains AI-free. Longitudinal change will require historical photos, so trial activation should occur when the user has suitable comparison photos.
- Measure free-to-paid conversion, retained paid subscribers, reports used, analysis quality failures, refunds and net contribution margin. Test $5.99 vs $7.99 and annual packaging before treating a recommended price as settled.

This note does not model AI/cloud costs, store commissions, taxes, payment setup or clinical validation; those require separate analysis. No app code was modified.
